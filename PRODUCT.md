# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: React + Vite with a lightweight CSS design system, chosen for a fast, static, SEO-friendly commercial website; shadcn/ui guidance informs component composition where useful.

## Users

Pet guardians in São Paulo, especially in and around Vila da Saúde, looking for veterinary care, specialists, grooming, pet shop services or supportive therapies for dogs and cats.

## Product Purpose

The public site explains the clinic and pet shop's services, specialties, practical differentiators and location, then converts visitors into consultations and direct conversations through WhatsApp, Instagram and directions. Success means a visitor can understand the offer within seconds and take a contact action without friction.

## Positioning

A single local destination combining veterinary care, specialist referrals, pet shop, grooming and sensory care for dogs and cats, presented with warmth and professional clarity.

## Operating Context

Visitors may arrive from local search or social media on a phone, often needing an answer quickly: what is offered, where the clinic is, and how to book or ask a question.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One public, content-led landing page with anchored navigation.
- Working links for WhatsApp, Instagram and Google Maps.
- Exact supplied service and specialty names must be preserved.
- Do not invent awards, certifications, numbers, testimonials or years of experience.
- Future real assets include clinic photos, facade, logo and existing brand materials; current pet photography must be easy to replace.
- Must be responsive from mobile through large desktop with no horizontal overflow.
- Microinteractions only: discreet hover, fade-in and transitions; support reduced motion.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Vira Lata Vira Amor.
- Existing direction: blue, yellow, turquoise and white; curved organic forms; dog and cat elements; cheerful, welcoming and professional.
- Voice: Brazilian Portuguese, natural, caring, confident and commercially clear without AI clichés.
- Primary calls to action: Agendar consulta, Falar pelo WhatsApp and Como chegar.

## Evidence on Hand

The user supplied the full content brief and factual contact/location links. No clinic photography, facade, logo file or current visual materials have been provided yet. Those absent assets must not be fabricated as real clinic evidence.

## Product Principles

1. Make the next useful action obvious.
2. Show breadth without making the page feel like a directory.
3. Use warmth as a proof of care, not as a substitute for clarity.
4. Keep local facts, services and specialties easy to scan.
5. Preserve replaceability until the clinic's real assets arrive.
